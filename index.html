About Me

WHO IS
THIS GUY?

Hey! I'm Sahil Arya — a UX/UI Designer with 1.5 years of experience making digital products that don't make people want to throw their phones across the room. That's a win, honestly.

I'm obsessed with clean UI, silky animations, and actually understanding users before slapping pixels on a screen. Based in Chandigarh, India, working with startups and studios to build things that look as good as they feel.

When I'm not designing, I'm probably judging your app's onboarding flow in my head. Don't take it personally.
